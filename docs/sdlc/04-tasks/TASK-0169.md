# TASK-0169: Referral Attribution and Qualification Foundation

## Outcome

Sprint 46 has a tenant-scoped referral record that can be captured once during
tenant registration and evaluated deterministically for qualification. This
slice records the referral lifecycle and audit fields; it does not grant or
consume bonus quota.

## Acceptance criteria

1. An active tenant can retrieve one stable referral code; codes are unique and
   are not shared across tenants.
2. Registration attribution is immutable and idempotent. Self-referrals,
   duplicate attribution to another referrer, circular referrals, disabled or
   unknown codes, and cross-tenant misuse are rejected.
3. Referral records support `clicked`, `attributed`, `pending`, `qualified`,
   `rejected`, and `reversed` states with source, reason, actor, and timestamps.
4. Qualification is idempotent and succeeds only when the referred tenant is
   active, KYC is approved, and at least one package order is paid without a
   voided payment document.
5. Referral operations never mutate package limits, entitlements, or usage
   counters. Bonus quota ledger/grants are deferred to a later Sprint 46 task.
6. Tenant endpoints expose only the authenticated tenant's code/referrals;
   qualification is platform-admin protected.
